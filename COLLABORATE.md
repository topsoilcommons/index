# Collaborate on the Index

The Index gives a community one public record in time order, checked by named humans. This page lists concrete ways to work with it. Nothing here implies an existing partnership.

## Who this is for

Community groups, schools, local archives, newsrooms, mutual aid groups, and public offices that keep records other people need to trust.

## Sized tasks

1. **Run a pilot index.** Fork this repository, keep the same format, and file your own records for one month under your own hands.
2. **Check entries in the open.** Pick an entry, reopen its original, and submit the check with your name and date.
3. **Adopt the format.** Use format.json in your own record system, and report where it fails in real use.

## What you get

A public, dated record whose entries carry their originals, fingerprints, seals, states, and checks. Anyone can mirror it and prove that a copy is complete.

## Start

Open an issue in this repository. Small and specific beats grand and vague.
